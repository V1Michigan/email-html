🚢 Ship-It Sunday

You’ve got ideas in your head. You need time and a space to build.

That’s where Ship-It Sundays come in—weekly sessions designed to give you the space, resources, and community to create. Whether you’re brainstorming alongside friends, shipping a feature, or putting the final polish on your project, this is your open sandbox to build as obsessively as you desire.

The only rule? No homework allowed.

P.S. food’s on us—because good ideas (and late-night debugging) require good fuel.

The culmination of V1 Community’s building real-world products from scratch.
